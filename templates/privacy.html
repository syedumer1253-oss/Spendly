Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Introduction

Spendly ("we", "us", "our", or "Company") operates the Spendly application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, including your name, email address, and password. When you use Spendly, we automatically collect information about your expense data, including amounts, dates, categories, and descriptions you enter.

We also collect information about how you access and use the application, such as your browser type, operating system, referring URLs, and pages visited. This helps us improve our service and understand user behavior patterns.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve Spendly's functionality. Your expense data is used to generate reports, analytics, and visualizations specific to your account. We use your email address to send important service updates and respond to your inquiries.

We may use aggregated and de-identified data for research, analytics, and marketing purposes. This data cannot be used to identify you personally and helps us improve our service for all users.

Data Security

We take the security of your personal data seriously and implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You use Spendly at your own risk and assume responsibility for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services to enhance functionality and user experience. These may include analytics providers, hosting services, and payment processors. We only share the minimum information necessary for these services to operate.

These third-party service providers are contractually obligated to use your information only for the purpose of providing services to us. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact us. Your privacy concerns are important to us, and we're committed to addressing them promptly.
